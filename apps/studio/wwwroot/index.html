Why these settings?

Brightness is a shelf on top of the matched tone — the match can only give you the reference's top end, not more of it. Width spreads everything above 250 Hz and leaves the bass centred, so it survives a mono system. Extra headroom sits further under the reference; the master already stops short of over-driving its own limiter.
